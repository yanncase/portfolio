Research project · TIPE · MP preparatory classes

Predicting cardiac fibrillation with limit cycles

S
TIPE, the research project of French preparatory classes, assessed by a jury in the entrance exams. Mostly individual, with one part carried out in pairs. Research question: how can the study of limit cycles and bifurcations anticipate the shift from a regular heart rhythm to a dangerous arrhythmia?
T
Understand and prove the period-doubling (flip) bifurcation that makes the action potential duration alternate, a warning sign of ventricular fibrillation.
A
Read and summarised research papers (Quail, Berger, Karagueuzian). Studied the bifurcation criterion on the restitution map. Simulated the Mitchell-Schaeffer cardiac model in Python and plotted bifurcation diagrams. Proved an early-warning result based on the autocorrelation of the signal (AR(1) process).
R
The proof succeeded. Graded 14/20: the jury found the presentation carried too much information. Main lesson: select what matters and simplify the message for the audience.

Mathematical modelling · dynamical systems · Python · numerical simulation · time series · scientific literature

ENSEEIHT · 1st year course

Automated proofs with Why3

S
First-year course at ENSEEIHT introducing formal methods.
T
Learn to use Why3, a platform that checks the correctness of programs and theorems by sending proof goals to automated provers or to the Coq proof assistant.
A
Wrote specifications and axioms, then let the provers check the proof obligations.
R
I was struck that a computer can prove a theorem on its own once it is given enough axioms. It links my taste for mathematics with computer science.

Formal methods · logic · Why3 · Coq

My most formative experience

On my way to becoming an engineer, my most formative experience is probably the TIPE. It made me reach a level of research, synthesis and understanding I had never reached before: reading research papers, proving a result on my own, and building simulations to check it. Since part of the work was done in pairs, it also gave me a first taste of a group project.

What I need to improve

The jury's feedback on my TIPE was clear: I put too much information in my presentation. I need to learn to prioritise, to keep only what serves the message, and to adapt it to my audience. This is a skill I will need as an engineer, in front of a client or a team.

Why AI and entrepreneurship

AI is the tool that is changing the world as a whole. Having real technical knowledge of it, and not only using it, seems to me a major asset for an engineer. Entrepreneurship is the way to turn ideas into reality: it is what makes it possible to go from an idea to something concrete that people use. Combining the two is what I would like to build my career on.
